Tata Motors anchors its enterprise sustainability strategy within the Tata Group's overarching Project Aalingana framework, embedding environmental, social, and governance principles into its operational lifecycle. The company has committed to achieving Net Zero greenhouse gas emissions across Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 by 2040 for its Passenger Vehicle business and by 2045 for its Commercial Vehicle operations. To transition its manufacturing footprint, Tata Motors aims to achieve 100% renewable electricity procurement across all manufacturing plants by 2030, currently scaling rooftop solar capacities, wind energy power purchase agreements, and captive installations. In parallel, Tata Motors accelerates product decarbonization by leading the Indian electric vehicle market and engineering alternate propulsion systems including hydrogen internal combustion engines and hydrogen fuel cell electric vehicles.

Resource efficiency and circular economy practices form the core operational pillars of environmental stewardship at Tata Motors. The company has pioneered registered vehicle scrapping facilities branded as Re.Wi.Re (Recycle with Respect) to dismantle and recycle end-of-life commercial and passenger vehicles systematically. Through its Tata Prolife remanufacturing initiative, key automotive aggregates such as engines, gearboxes, and power-steering units are restored to original equipment manufacturer specifications, conserving virgin raw materials and reducing lifecycle emissions. Operationally, Tata Motors targets Zero Waste to Landfill across all Indian manufacturing facilities and Water Positivity by 2030 through zero liquid discharge plants, closed-loop industrial wastewater recycling, and watershed replenishment.

The Corporate Social Responsibility framework of Tata Motors functions under Section 135 of the Companies Act 2013, deploying targeted programs across four primary focus areas: Health, Education, Employability, and Environment. Under the Aarogya health pillar, the company addresses severe acute malnutrition in children aged zero to six, provides maternal prenatal and postnatal care, and operates mobile medical units in underserved peri-urban and rural localities. The Vidyadhanam education program supports secondary and higher secondary students through scholarships, digital infrastructure, and remedial coaching for high-stakes examinations to curb school dropout rates in marginalized demographics.

For socio-economic empowerment, the Kaushalya initiative delivers automotive mechanic and technical trade training in collaboration with Industrial Training Institutes and specialized Learn-and-Earn models, driving gainful placement rates across the automotive supply chain. The Vasundhara environmental CSR program focuses on large-scale urban and rural afforestation with indigenous saplings to create local carbon sinks and restore degraded biodiversity corridors. These community initiatives intentionally incorporate the Aadhaar Affirmative Action framework—which ensures over 40% of CSR beneficiaries belong to Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe communities—while the Amrutdhara program provides potable drinking water security to drought-prone villages, complemented by structured employee volunteering through the Seva platform.
